HOW TO PLAY

1
🛡
SAFE ROOM FIRST
Every level opens with a hazard-free view of the warehouse — no timer, no scoring. Look around and get familiar, or skip straight in.
2
🖱
LOOK AROUND FREELY
Drag to look around, W/S to walk, A/D to turn, or click the floor to walk there — a full 3D warehouse you can explore.
3
⏱
BEAT THE CLOCK
Once you begin, you have a fixed time limit for the whole level — find enough hazards before it runs out.
4
⚠
SPOT IT...
Click any hazard, anytime, in any order — there's no rush per hazard, just the overall level timer.
5
✔
...THEN FIX IT
Choose the correct real response — Report it or Fix it now. Picking the wrong one costs points, even if you spotted the hazard itself.
6
🏁
PASS OR REPLAY
Each level needs a minimum number of hazards resolved to pass. Fall short and you'll replay the same level until you get there.
7
📈
LEVELS GET HARDER
Passing a level unlocks the next one. Later levels bring different hazard mixes and tighter pass requirements — the same warehouse, tougher shifts.
8
🏆
SCORE & IMPROVE
Your final score breaks down early/late catches, missed hazards, and fix choices — so you see exactly where to improve next run.

Resolve at least 4 hazards before time runs out to pass a level — fewer, and you'll replay it.
